About the property

Description property

Lett-Duggan Properties Ltd are delighted to present to the market “Journey’s End”.

This deceptively spacious detached four bedroom family home is on a generous sized plot that enjoys an abundance of natural light. There is great privacy afforded to the rear garden which is sheltered by mature planting.

The accommodation comprises of living room, kitchen – dining area, utility room, family bathroom and a downstairs bedroom. Upstairs there are three bedrooms with master bedroom ensuite.

This property also has the added benefit of two stone built out buildings.

Situated on the outskirts of Ballyedmond Village, “Journey’s End” enjoys a tranquil setting in a stunning rural area of North Co Wexford. The property is a short drive to M11 motorway.
